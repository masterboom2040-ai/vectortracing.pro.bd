FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between manual and auto vector tracing?

Auto-tracing uses software algorithms (like Illustrator's Image Trace) which often produce messy paths, unnecessary anchor points, and jagged edges. Manual vector tracing uses the pen tool by a skilled human artist — every path is intentionally drawn, resulting in clean, optimized, print-ready vectors.

What file formats do you provide?

All projects include AI (Adobe Illustrator), EPS, SVG, PDF, and high-resolution PNG files. Embroidery-specific formats (DST, PES, JEF, VIP) and vinyl cutter formats (DXF) are available on request.

How long does vector tracing take?

Standard delivery is 24 hours. Complex multi-layered artwork may take 48 hours. Express same-day delivery is available for an additional fee on simple projects.

How many revisions do you offer?

Unlimited revisions are offered until you are completely satisfied. Your satisfaction is the priority — no extra charges for revision rounds.

What quality of image do you need from me?

Any quality is accepted — even very low-resolution, blurry, or damaged images. The lower the original quality, the more artistic judgment is applied. Clear photos of hand-drawn sketches work perfectly.

Can you match exact Pantone or brand colors?

Yes. Simply provide the Pantone, CMYK, RGB, or HEX color codes and they will be matched exactly in the vector file. Brand guidelines are always followed precisely.
